Privacy Policy

Lensii is a camera app. This policy explains what it does and doesn't do with your data — in plain terms, because there isn't much to explain: Lensii is built local-first. Your photos, videos, and everything you capture stay on your device unless you explicitly choose to share or save them elsewhere.

What Lensii collects

Nothing, by default. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in. Lensii doesn't ask who you are.
  • No analytics or tracking. There's no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no usage telemetry sent anywhere.
  • No server. Lensii has no backend of its own — there's nowhere for your data to go even if it wanted to.

Camera, microphone, and photo library

Lensii asks for camera and microphone access to let you take photos and record video — that's the whole app. Captures are saved to Lensii's private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Tapping "Save to Photos" is the only way a capture leaves Lensii's private storage — that's an explicit action you take, never automatic.

Screen recording

The Screen tool uses Android's own system screen-recording permission (MediaProjection), which shows Android's own consent dialog and a persistent recording indicator the entire time it's active — Lensii cannot start or hide screen recording without that. Recordings are saved locally, same as camera captures.

AR features (Trace)

The Trace tool uses ARCore, a Google Play Services component that runs entirely on your device to track surfaces via your camera. Lensii doesn't send anything from this feature to its own servers (it has none); Google Play Services for AR operates under Google's own privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Lensii never sees your payment details — only a purchase confirmation from Google Play, used to unlock what you paid for on your device.

Location

Lensii does not collect or request your location.

Data you can delete

Everything Lensii stores is under your control from inside the app — Settings has a "Clear all in-app media" action, and uninstalling the app removes everything Lensii ever stored.

Children's privacy

Lensii is not directed at children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect any data from anyone, of any age — there's simply no collection mechanism in the app to begin with.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the "Effective" date above will change accordingly.
